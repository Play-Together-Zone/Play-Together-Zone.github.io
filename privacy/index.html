Privacy & Open-Source Credits

Play-Together-Zone is a static, serverless web application hosted with zero backend database or tracking infrastructure. Need to reach the creator? Visit khushwinder-dev.github.io.

1. No Accounts & No Server Storage

We do not require registration, logins, or cookies. Your display name, avatar choice, persistent random player ID, theme preference, sound setting, blocked peer IDs, and win/loss counts are saved strictly in your browser's localStorage.

2. WebRTC Peer-to-Peer IP Address Disclosure

Multiplayer connections run directly between participants' browsers over WebRTC. Establishing a peer-to-peer WebRTC connection exchanges ICE network candidates, which means room participants' IP addresses and connection metadata may be visible to other peers in the room, as well as to the public STUN/TURN servers and signaling relays (WebTorrent trackers, Nostr relays, or MQTT brokers) used to negotiate the connection. Only join rooms with people you trust, or use a VPN / relay-only TURN configuration if you wish to mask your IP address. Optional voice and video chat streams are also transmitted directly peer-to-peer only when you explicitly turn on your microphone or camera, and are never recorded or sent to an application server.
